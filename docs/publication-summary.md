# Publication summary

The corrected study retains the question of useful PS16 film information at a conventional compressed RAW61 byte budget. The rendered route uses twelve primary film frames, exact retained bytes, native crop measurements and separate color/structure screens. Secondary captures and reduced-frame diagnostics are explicitly separated.

The equally prominent DNG route assesses a separate 200 MiB target on sixteen historical sources. Its technical result remains useful, while image-quality superiority over RAW61 remains unresolved. The former camera-RGB DeltaE and archive-value verdict are withdrawn.

The report was first approved by the owner and [published](https://hsnilsson.github.io/bayer-raw-vs-pixel-shift-jpeg-xl/) on 2026-09-17. Current numerical claims come from its [release manifest](../site/data/release.json). The results describe the retained rendering workflow and measured crops; the report's research questions identify the work needed for broader archival guidance.
