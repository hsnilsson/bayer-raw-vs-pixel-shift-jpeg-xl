# PixelShift combiner audit

The [corrected combiner section](../site/index.html#combiner) and [evidence JSON](../site/data/combiner-evidence.json) supersede the former gamma-domain measurements and latitude-winner terminology.

Two retained sequences and five fixed crops compare PixelShift2DNG and Sony/IED neutral renders with a newly rendered first-source ARW anchor. Actual ICC profiles, linear-light resampling, registration, common valid support and scalar midtone exposure matching are recorded. A 9×9 low-pass measures tonal agreement; a separate 5×5 high-pass correlation describes detail agreement.

The anchor is a noisy lower-resolution observation. Closeness to it is not evidence that a combiner recovered more capture latitude. The independent [controlled exposure experiment](controlled-exposure-latitude.md) remains a separate question. No new combiner investigation or capture was added in this repair.
