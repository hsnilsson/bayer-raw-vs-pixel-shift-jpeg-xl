# Findings and evidence map

The primary question remains useful film information at a conventional compressed RAW61 storage budget. The [current report](site/index.html) contains the generated numerical conclusions and all denominators.

| Evidence | What it measures | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rendered RGB16 JXL, 12 primary film frames | Exact per-frame RAW budget; native color and high-pass agreement with PS16 | Scene truth, isolated sampling gain, universal invisibility |
| Four additional rendered frames | Two secondary Gold frames, flat field, same-sequence f/4.5 target | An independent compressed-RAW primary size comparison |
| Historical DNG, 16 sources | 200 MiB budget, metadata, decode/application acceptance, selected camera-code errors | Display DeltaE or superiority over a rendered RAW61 |
| Six public TIFFs × four distances | Reproducible ICC-aware codec and edit sensitivity | PS16 sampling advantage |
| Two combiner sequences, five crops | Linear-light tonal agreement and detail correlation against an ARW anchor | Recovered capture latitude |
| Four controlled exposure brackets | Sensor-domain exposure and clipping behavior | A ranking of combiners or rendered JXL quality |

The d=2.0 controls satisfy the relative screens despite visible degradation in inspected crops. A favorable relative comparison therefore does not establish an archival-acceptability threshold; continuous error measurements, controls and visual review remain necessary.

The correction preserves original precision and actual ICC transfer curves, fixes unstable filtering and reference reuse, separates native and reduced scopes, and removes the old 5% size tolerance. Every current table and viewer label is bound to the same content-verified release. Historical findings in Git history or dated research notes must not be treated as current measurements.
