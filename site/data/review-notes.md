These observations are specific to the inspected crop and display; they do not establish a universal visibility threshold.

Fuji shadow recovery reported 88.59% reference white and 88.61% d=0.25 candidate white in the tested view, consistent with its mostly white appearance. These are display endpoints, not sensor clipping.

The strong-loss control softened fine texture while retaining larger boundaries. A native-pixel control and scale readout were added; the one-source-pixel-per-display-pixel view was checked at 100%.
