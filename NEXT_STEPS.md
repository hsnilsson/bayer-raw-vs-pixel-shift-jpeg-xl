# Remaining work

The repair delivers a validated local report and reproducible evidence for owner review. Publication is a separate decision; no push or deployment is included.

Before publication, the owner should inspect the corrected visual comparisons and conclusions. A publication instruction can then release the reviewed commit through the existing Pages workflow.

Further scientific work is optional and outside this repair: a validated common-render DNG/RAW bridge; calibrated application-specific inversion tests; independent human or blinded review; additional film stocks and exposures; stronger capture-color calibration; and new captures that isolate sampling from other workflow differences. The unresolved DNG quality advantage is explicitly stated, rather than filled with the withdrawn cross-domain score.

A production archive workflow would additionally require operational policies for metadata, application compatibility, independent backups, integrity monitoring and source retention. This report does not implement a collection-wide ingestion or deletion system.
